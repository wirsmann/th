Vibration bursts

In tease mode, the one in control can now and then narrow the stroke to this width in the middle of your range and run it at your full speed slider, so it buzzes instead of strokes. Then she rests and your range comes back. How often and how long is set per personality under Personas.

Your speed slider is the maximum, and the low and peak values below are percentages of it. While it's on, it replaces the ramp and pattern. The stroke range, speed cap, end slowdown, and time limits still apply. While a tease session is running, stopping or pausing counts as failing it: the device still stops right away, but you're locked out as if the time had run out.

Her settings

Pick a personality above to see and change her settings.

Gives up control: how likely she is to hand control over when someone asks, or on her own after a few minutes. Vibration length: how long her buzz lasts. Moves: her chance per tease cycle of each special move, with the vibration burst among them. Burst width and the on/off switch are under Session. Tease timing is what Tease mode loads into the Tease panel when she is picked; change it here and it is remembered for her.

Behaviour

Whoever is in control keeps a target speed and stroke length and nudges you toward it 5 points at a time. Each demand has Do it, Match her target and Refuse buttons. Refusing makes her more insistent. Questions come with answer buttons, and a few of the answers change what happens next (a rest, a hold, a fresh build-up).

Friends

Everyone here is on your friends list. Invite anyone at any time. Each one answers in her own time: she may accept, decline, be busy or offline, or not reply at all. A friend who didn't reply can still come in later, more easily while the room has space. How often she accepts, and an optional busy message, are set under Personas.

Your speed slider is the top speed. The ramp start, slow phase and end slowdown percentages are under Customize.

Lounge

A bigger crowd makes personalities challenge each other more and fight harder to keep control. With the second box on, it also nudges tease sessions: peaks up to 20% higher (never above your speed slider), rests up to 30% shorter, and up to 10 points more fake-outs.

Personalities who want control either ask you (you get Grant / Deny buttons) or ask the one in control directly (she decides, using her number under Personas).

Display

Press Esc at any time to stop immediately. The speed cap limits every mode.

After that much running time, the session pauses, the device stops and moves to the park position, and the clock freezes. Press Resume when you're ready. Breaks don't count as failing, and they don't use up session time. No break starts in the last minute and a half. If 0% parks at the wrong end for you, try 100.

While a cooldown or the daily limit is active, Start is disabled and the limit boxes are locked. By default a failed session only counts the time you actually ran, plus the lockout. This lives in your browser, so clearing site data or using another browser bypasses it.
